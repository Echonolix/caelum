This module vendors the fixed OpenAL API declaration headers
include/AL/al.h and include/AL/alc.h from OpenAL Soft 1.25.2, commit
b2c48f7718ef3fcf67921a8b6534c4914e328970.

Both vendored header files state that they are released under the Unlicense
(public-domain dedication). Their complete upstream notice follows and is also
retained at the top of each vendored header.

This notice applies specifically to the two vendored declaration headers. It
does not represent the licensing of OpenAL Soft as a whole; see the upstream
OpenAL Soft repository's COPYING file for its project-level licensing terms.

codegen/openal-core.h is a mechanically normalized generator input derived
from exactly those two headers. It retains the OpenAL 1.1 AL and ALC core
surface while removing parser-irrelevant include guards, C++ linkage blocks,
and declaration-only platform attributes.
