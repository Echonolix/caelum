The archive README describes the _res* files as Stanford-provided decimated
versions suitable for interactive rendering. The decimation is crude and may
introduce small holes or fail to preserve topology.

Stanford's repository terms require source acknowledgement. They welcome use
for research purposes and free mirroring/redistribution, but prohibit commercial
use or inclusion in a product for sale without permission. Credit:

    Stanford University Computer Graphics Laboratory

The repository also notes that the dragon is a symbol of Chinese culture and
asks that renderings and other uses remain in good taste. This sample keeps the
model static apart from a conventional turntable view and does not deform,
damage, break, melt, or otherwise mistreat the cultural artifact.
